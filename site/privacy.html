Privacy Policy

Applies to the Keo desktop app, Keo Cloud (Keo’s online service) and this website. Last updated 26 November 2026.

The short version

Keo runs on your computer, and your conversations, memories, projects and files stay there. When you use Keo’s own AI with a Keo account, what Keo needs to answer you passes through Keo Cloud to the AI model and back — Keo Cloud does not keep it. Keo Cloud stores only your account, your plan, your usage counts and your payments. If you use your own AI key instead, your messages go straight from your computer to that provider. We never sell your information and never use your conversations to train AI.

What stays on your PC

  • Conversations, memories, projects, tasks, Studio projects, builds, the audit log and settings — in your Windows profile (%APPDATA%\Keo).
  • Passwords and keys you give Keo — email app passwords, your Google sign-in, your own AI provider keys, connected accounts — encrypted with Windows’ own data protection. Keo never shows them again.
  • Finding files (only if you turn it on): Keo looks file names up in Windows’ own search index on your PC. The names and paths it finds — and the text of a file (including PDFs), only if you also allow reading — go to your AI to answer you.
  • Photo descriptions (only if you turn on “Let Keo look at my photos”): a one-sentence description of each picture Keo looked at, kept on your PC so you can find photos by what is in them. You can delete them in Settings → Files.
  • Screen captures you take in Keo — kept for 24 hours by default (adjustable), then deleted.
  • Wake-word audio (“Hey Keo”) — processed by Windows speech recognition on your PC and discarded.

Your Keo account

When you create a Keo account, Keo Cloud stores in its account database:

  • your email address and a securely hashed password (we never see your password);
  • your plan, until when it is paid, and its renewal status;
  • usage counts — AI requests, tokens, web searches, pictures, voice seconds and Studio credits, and what the AI services charged us — so we can apply your plan’s allowance;
  • payment records: plan, amount, currency, date and Paystack’s references;
  • problem reports you choose to send from Keo (Diagnostics → Report a problem).

Keo Cloud does not store the content of your conversations, searches, pictures, videos or recordings.

What passes through Keo Cloud

When you use Keo’s AI with your Keo account, these pass through Keo Cloud to the service that does the work, and back. Keo Cloud passes them on and does not keep them:

  • Messages — what you send, the conversation so far, and what you or Keo add to it (attached pictures or document text, results from Keo’s tools, web pages Keo read for you, things on your screen you asked about). These go through OpenRouter to the company that runs the AI model (for example OpenAI, Google or Anthropic).
  • Web searches through Keo Search — through OpenRouter to its search partner.
  • Pictures, video, music, sound and 3D models you ask Keo to make or change — your description and the pictures or clips being changed go to fal.ai and the model it runs.
  • Voice — text Keo reads aloud with Keo’s voice, and recordings you choose to send for speech-to-text.

Those companies handle what they receive under their own privacy terms and may keep it for a limited time, for example for safety and abuse monitoring.

Other things that leave your PC, only when you use them

  • Your own AI provider (OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, or none for a local model), if you use your own key instead of a Keo account: your messages and the context Keo includes go directly to them.
  • Brave Search, if you add a key: your search queries.
  • OpenAI speech-to-text, only if you turn it on with your own key: recorded voice commands.
  • GitHub: once a day Keo checks GitHub for a new version and downloads it in the background. No personal data is sent; you can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & data.
  • WhatsApp: messages you approve (or allow in a live chat) are sent from your own WhatsApp, as if you typed them.
  • Your email provider, if you connect an account: Keo signs in to your own mail server to read, search and draft mail when you ask, and sends only messages you approved.
  • YouTube, Telegram, Notion, Slack and Google Drive, only if you connect them: what you ask Keo to post or save there.
  • Websites Keo opens for you — for example a site you asked Keo to study or a page it fills in with your OK.

Google Calendar (Google user data)

  • What Keo asks for: only the Google Calendar events permission (calendar.events) — to see your events, find free time, and add, change or delete events. Not your Gmail, contacts, Drive or anything else.
  • How it is used: only to do what you ask Keo in the app — for example “what’s on my calendar tomorrow?” or “book a meeting on Friday at 3”. Adding, changing and deleting events always asks you first, and inviting people always asks.
  • Where it goes: calendar data comes straight from Google to your PC. To answer you, the events relevant to your request are sent to your AI (see above), as part of that one request. It is not stored on Keo Cloud, not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.
  • Storage: Keo keeps only the sign-in (encrypted on your PC), not a copy of your calendar. Events you discussed stay in that conversation’s history on your PC until you delete it.
  • Removing access: Settings → Email & calendar → Sign out (Keo also revokes the access at Google), or at any time in your Google Account → Security → Your connections to third-party apps.
  • Keo’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Payments

Payments are handled by Paystack. You type your card details into Paystack’s secure checkout; Keo never sees or stores your card number. Paystack tells us whether a payment succeeded, the amount and plan, and its references.

This website and the early-access list

  • If you join the early-access list, we keep your email address, the device you picked (Windows or iPhone), the page you signed up from, and a one-way scrambled form of your internet address (used only to stop spam sign-ups — it cannot be turned back into the address).
  • We use your email only to send your invite and important news about Keo’s launch.
  • This website uses no cookies, no trackers and no analytics.

Who we work with

  • Supabase — Keo accounts and the account database.
  • Cloudflare — hosts Keo Cloud and handles Keo’s email addresses.
  • OpenRouter and the AI companies it connects to — AI answers, search and voice.
  • fal.ai and the models it runs — pictures, video, music, sound and 3D.
  • Paystack — payments.
  • Resend — sends Keo’s account and invite emails.
  • GitHub — hosts this website and Keo’s updates.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your conversations to train AI models.

How long we keep it

We keep your account details while you have an account, and your early-access email until you join Keo or ask us to remove it. Usage and payment records are kept as long as needed for billing, accounting and the law. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account details; payment records the law requires us to keep are kept for that time only.

Your control and your rights

  • Using your apps is off until you allow specific apps; actions ask for approval by default.
  • Delete any memory, conversation or capture — or everything — in the app (Settings → Privacy & data → Delete all data). Uninstalling keeps your data folder so a reinstall can continue; delete it in the app first if you want it gone.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or object to how we use it — including under Nigeria’s Data Protection Act 2023 and, where it applies, the GDPR.

Children

Keo is not meant for children under 13, and paid plans are for adults (or with a parent’s or guardian’s permission).

Security

Connections to Keo Cloud are encrypted. The keys that let Keo Cloud work stay in Cloudflare and are never sent to the app, and only Keo Cloud can change plans or usage in the account database.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update this page and its date; for important changes we tell you in Keo.

Contact

Questions, requests or problems: support@heykeo.com.
