Questions

Is it free?

Keo is free during the beta. With your own key you pay the AI provider for what you use; local models are free. Paid extras (like AI-made video or 3D models) show their price before they run.

Does Keo send my data to you?

With your own key, messages go only to the AI provider you connect. Your conversations, memory, settings and keys stay on your PC. Privacy note

Can Keo do things on my computer without asking?

Not by default. Using your apps is off until you allow specific apps. Payments, deleting, passwords and messaging people always ask, even in Full control.

Why does Windows warn about the installer?

The beta is not code-signed yet, so Windows does not recognise the publisher. Signed builds come with a later version.

How do updates work?

Keo updates itself: the new version downloads in the background and installs when you close Keo. Your data and settings are kept.
